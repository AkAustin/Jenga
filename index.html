Official Jenga Rules & 360° Controls

Authentic 54-block timber tower in 3 wood species (Maple, Cherry Oak, Walnut).

  • 1
    360° Parlor Table & Player Chairs: Drag the background, use two fingers on mobile, or use the Camera View bar to inspect all 4 sides of the tower and the chairs around the table.
  • 2
    3 Wood Colors & Block Tension: Blocks are crafted from Natural Maple, Honey Cherry Oak, and American Walnut. Tap or hover a block to check whether it sits Loose, Snug, or Tight.
  • 3
    Below the Top Completed Layer: You may only extract blocks from below the highest completed 3-block story.
  • 4
    Stacking & Stability Check: Once pulled out, tap one of the 3 perpendicular slots on top. The tower must remain standing for 1.1 seconds to complete your turn.
