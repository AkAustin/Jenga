Official Jenga Rules & 360° Controls

Authentic 54-block hardwood tower dimensions (7.5 × 2.5 × 1.5 ratio).

  • 1
    360° All-Around Inspection: Drag the background, use two fingers on mobile, or slide the bottom 360° Turntable Slider / ↺ ↻ buttons to inspect all 4 sides of the tower!
  • 2
    Probing for Loose Blocks: Each row has loose, snug, and tight load-bearing blocks. Tap or hover a block to check its tension!
  • 3
    Below the Top Completed Layer: You may only extract blocks from below the highest completed 3-block story.
  • 4
    Stacking & Stability Check: Once pulled out, click one of the 3 glowing perpendicular slots on top. The tower must remain standing for 1.1 seconds to complete your turn!
